Problem / Solution

Practical answers
to operational friction.

Orders are copied by hand between systems.
Automate the sync, with validation and visible errors.
Stock levels differ between channels.
Define a source of truth and keep inventory updates in sync.
Customers keep asking where their order is.
Send tracking and shipment updates back to the store and customer.
Reports and database queries are slow.
Tune SQL, indexing, and Entity Framework data access.
Integrations fail silently.
Add retries, logging, and alerts so failures can be resolved.
Older .NET software is risky to change.
Stabilize it and plan a step-by-step move to modern .NET.
The Shopify theme does not match how the business sells.
Build custom Liquid sections and JavaScript storefront features.
The team needs technical direction.
Provide hands-on leadership, reviews, and a clear delivery process.
